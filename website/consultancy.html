The Problem

Most operational problems
are invisible from inside.

When you are in the business every day, workarounds become normal. The late invoice, the re-typed spreadsheet, the approval stuck in someone's inbox: each one seems small, but together they set the pace of the whole business.

01

Workarounds that became the process

A spreadsheet here, a reminder there. Over time the workaround becomes the way things are done, and nobody remembers why.

02

Knowledge stuck in people's heads

When only one person knows how something works, every holiday or departure is a risk. The process needs to live in a system, not in a person.

03

Fixing symptoms, not causes

Late orders, errors, and overtime are symptoms. The cause is usually a handoff, a missing step, or a tool that doesn't fit the work. That has to be diagnosed first.

04

Buying software before understanding the problem

A new tool on top of a broken process just makes the broken process faster. We look at the workflow first, then decide whether you need software at all.

What We Look At

Six areas.
One clear picture.

Every review starts by understanding how work actually flows. From there we examine six areas to find where time, money, and accuracy are being lost.

Workflow Mapping

We sit down with the people doing the work and draw the real process, step by step, including the workarounds nobody wrote down.

  • Step-by-step process maps
  • Who does what, and when
  • Where work waits or gets repeated
  • Unwritten rules and workarounds

Bottleneck Diagnosis

We find the one or two points that limit how much your business can handle, and trace them back to their real cause.

  • Delays and rework
  • Capacity limits
  • Error hotspots
  • Root cause, not symptoms

Tools & Data

We look at the software, spreadsheets, and records you already use, and where information is re-typed, lost, or out of sync.

  • Current tools and how they are used
  • Duplicate data entry
  • Systems that don't connect
  • Gaps in what you can see

Roles & Handoffs

Work often stalls where it passes from one person or team to another. We clarify who owns what, and where responsibility is unclear.

  • Ownership of each step
  • Approval and sign-off points
  • Handoff delays
  • Accountability gaps

Cost of the Problem

We put numbers on what the bottleneck costs you, in hours, errors, delays, and lost revenue, so you can decide what is worth fixing first.

  • Time spent on manual work
  • Error and rework cost
  • Delays that affect customers
  • Priority order for fixes

Recommended Fix & Next Steps

You get a clear recommendation: a process change, a simple tool, or a custom system, with scope, cost estimate, and what success looks like.

  • Process change or software, whichever fits
  • Scope and cost estimate
  • Success measures
  • Optional build and ongoing support

Our Approach

Diagnosis before prescription.
Always.

We don't walk in with a pre-built answer. Every recommendation starts with understanding how your business actually runs, and that discipline is what makes the advice useful.

01

Listen & Map

We talk to the people who do the work, review the tools and records they use, and map how work really moves through the business. This gives us a picture that a manager's summary alone can't.

02

Find the Bottleneck

We identify where time, accuracy, or money is being lost, and trace each problem to its root cause. Everything we recommend is anchored to what we find here.

03

Design the Fix

We recommend a practical fix in plain business language, with scope, cost, and the result you should expect. It isn't a 60-slide deck. It's a clear plan with decisions, owners, and success measures.

04

Hand Over or Build

You get a written plan you can act on yourself. If you want us to build or set up the system, we scope that next, with agreed acceptance criteria, and we check it solves the original problem.
