How It Works

Diagnose. Design. Deliver.

1. Diagnose

We map how work really flows through your business and find where it gets stuck, repeated, or lost. You get a plain-language picture of the problem and what it costs you.

2. Design

We agree the fix with you before anything is built, in business terms: what changes, who uses it, and how we will know it worked.

3. Deliver

We build and set up the system, show your team how to use it, and stay on for support. Sometimes the fix is a simpler process, not new software, and we will say so.

A short-term rental compliance platform for BC strata councils, built because tracking it by hand is slow and error-prone. Booking approvals, night tracking, real-time notifications, and audit-ready reporting for strata councils.
